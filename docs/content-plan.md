# Ai News — episode format (locked Oct 31 2026)

## What it is
A **daily roundup of everything that happened in AI** in the last ~24 hours, in under 2 minutes.
Not one deep story — a full sweep, 1–2 lines per story (Uzair: "don't go in depth, give 1 or 2 lines so we can cover all the things").

## Why this format (Uzair's decisions, Oct 31)
- A roundup gives a reason to watch *every day* (completeness) — viewers trust they didn't miss anything.
- Same opening line every day = brand recognition and a ritual.
- On-screen table of contents tells viewers it's worth staying to the end.
- Max ~2 minutes keeps retention high while still covering everything.

## Structure (every episode)
1. **Fixed opening line:** "Here's everything that happened in AI today... in two minutes. Go."
2. **Today's Rundown TOC** on screen — the 3 categories, current one highlighted.
3. **01 · AI NEWS** — models, developer/agent news, security, business/funding, research. Big stories ~10 s, small ones ~5 s.
4. **02 · PRICING & FREE** — price cuts/hikes, plan changes, newly free tools/tiers/credits.
5. **03 · AI TOOLS** — new tools and product launches.
6. **Close:** "That was today's AI news in two minutes... subscribe, same time tomorrow."
Visual: navy/red anchor-desk studio, stick-figure anchor with lip-sync, story counter chip (n / total), category banner wipes between sections, one prop/scene per story (morphs, counters, kinetic numbers).

## Where the news comes from
- **Daily AI News Digest** — a scheduled research task on Uzair's account (runs 10:57 and 21:57 PKT). It searches the web and writes a report in 9 sections:
  1 New model launches · 2 New AI tools · 3 Free tools/free tiers · 4 Offers & deals · 5 Pricing & plan changes · 6 Developer & agentic AI news · 7 Creator tools · 8 Business & industry · 9 Research & open source.
- Mapping: sections 1, 6, 8, 9 → **AI NEWS**; 3, 4, 5 → **PRICING & FREE**; 2, 7 → **AI TOOLS**.
- Always re-verify the top stories with fresh searches and fetch the original source.
